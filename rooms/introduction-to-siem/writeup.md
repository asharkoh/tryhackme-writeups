# Introduction to System Information and Event Management

**Platform:** TryHackMe  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Date:** 1/7/26

## What the room covers
This room covered the basics of a Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) dashboard,
including what SIEM stands for and its role in monitoring and analysing security events.

## What I did
I explored an emulated SIEM dashboard on the TryHackMe website to investigate a security alert. 
I identified the suspicious activity that triggered the alert, located the rule responsible for detecting it,
and determined whether the alert was a true positive or a false positive.
## What I learned
I learned how SIEM dashboards use detection rules to identify suspicious activity and gained a better 
understanding of how security alerts are investigated and validated.
