# Introduction to System Information and Event Management

**Platform:** TryHackMe
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Date:** 1/7/26

## What the room covers

This room introduces the role of SIEM platforms in a Security Operations Centre (SOC).
It explains how Level 1 SOC analysts investigate alerts, assess threats, and escalate incidents when required.

## What I did

I investigated a security alert using a SIEM dashboard and analysed the associated event data. I performed a reputation check on the source IP address,
identified that it had been linked to four previous attacks, and documented the findings before escalating the incident to a senior SOC analyst for further investigation.

## What I learned

I learned how a Level 1 SOC analyst investigates security alerts and uses a SIEM platform to support incident response.
I also gained a better understanding of when and why incidents should be escalated.
